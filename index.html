The festival

Nuakhai is the first taste
of the new harvest.

Across western Odisha, Nuakhai marks the moment the season's first grain reaches the household — offered first in gratitude, then eaten together. What follows is Nuakhai Juhar: the round of greetings where elders are sought out, differences are set down, and the year starts clean.

Bhetghat is our version of that in Bengaluru — a meeting-place for Aghria families who live far from the fields but not from the festival. Food from home, our own songs and dances on stage, and children who get to hear Sambalpuri spoken by more than just their parents.

  • Traditional Sambalpuri spread
  • Cultural programmes by our own families
  • Games and activities for children
  • Nuakhai Juhar with the elders
